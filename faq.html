FAQs

Frequently asked questions

A few common questions about working with us in Bulgaria. This list will grow.

Do you work with international clients?

Yes. We look after bookkeeping, VAT, payroll and annual reporting for Bulgarian and internationally owned companies, and we work mainly in English. Documents can be exchanged digitally, so most routine accounting can be handled remotely.

Can you help us register a company in Bulgaria?

Yes. We can guide you through the company registration process and related administrative steps, helping you understand the requirements for your business. Contact us to discuss your plans and the support you need.

What accounting and tax services do you provide?

Our services include bookkeeping, VAT and tax consulting, payroll, annual accounting, management reports and administrative support. We can tailor the support to your company’s needs.
